# THΛTJOINT Rollout Strategy & Geographic Markets

## 03 / Rollout Framework
Rollout is structured around three deliberate stages, ensuring software adoption aligns directly with the physical operation:

1. **01 / LISTEN — Find the friction**:
   - Audit where on-site operations teams lose context, duplicate spreadsheets, or wait on communication handoffs.
   - Map existing vendor payment practices and resident communication touchpoints.
2. **02 / CONFIGURE — Shape the workspace**:
   - Align property hierarchies, room typologies, roles, access controls, and banking rails.
   - Ingest historic leases, tenant KYC registries, and maintenance history.
3. **03 / LEARN — Begin deliberately**:
   - Initiate with a single building or cluster pilot.
   - Review operational signals over a 30-day billing cycle before portfolio-wide expansion.
