# THΛTJOINT Platform Modules & Operating Architecture

## 01 / Overview
THΛTJOINT connects rent, residents, repairs, and documents into one calm, unified operating system for modern rental portfolios. Regardless of whether an operator manages individual villas, dense student communities, co-living clusters, or cross-border portfolios, THΛTJOINT provides different operational views connected to a single continuous property ledger.

## Module 01: Single-Family Homes
*Every home. One operating rhythm.*
- **Scope**: Geared towards independent landlords, boutique rental owners, and scattered single-family residential portfolios.
- **Address-Attached Ledger**: Connects the physical home address directly with resident lease history, repair logs, rent status, and statutory documents.
- **Key Capabilities**:
  1. **Home + Resident Sync**: Full visibility from move-in inspection to renewal agreements.
  2. **Rent & Repairs Pipeline**: Automated maintenance ticketing linked directly to the tenant portal; vendors receive scoped work orders.
  3. **Renewal Context**: Historic maintenance spend, payment punctuality, and market rent adjustments available at lease expiry.

## Module 02: Communities & Co-Living
*Beds, buildings, and shared rhythms.*
- **Scope**: Purpose-built student accommodations (PBSA), high-density urban co-living, and shared residential apartments.
- **Inventory Granularity**: Manages inventory down to the bed and room level (Single Rooms, Double Single-Occupancy, Double Shared).
- **Key Capabilities**:
  1. **Room & Bed Allocation**: Real-time room inventory management preventing double-booking across shared apartments.
  2. **Resident Communities & Directory**: Digital KYC dossiers, roommate matching preferences, shared utility billing, and common area amenity scheduling.
  3. **Add-on Services**: In-app meal plans, laundry subscription add-ons, and late-night food delivery webhooks.

## Module 03: Short-Term & Hybrid Rentals
*Turnover without friction.*
- **Scope**: Serviced apartments, corporate housing, and hybrid flexible-stay properties.
- **Turnover Orchestration**:
  1. **Check-in Automation**: Contactless digital check-in with identity verification before arrival.
  2. **Cleaning & Inspection Routines**: Dynamic dispatch of housekeeping teams upon checkout signal.
  3. **Access Control**: Dynamic time-gated digital lock PIN generation per guest reservation.

## Module 04: Portfolio Operations
*Executive clarity across towers, cities, and corridors.*
- **Scope**: Real estate holding groups, asset managers, and institutional property developers.
- **Multi-Building Consolidation**:
  1. **Multi-Currency Revenue**: Real-time aggregation of collections across GCC (AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR) and India (INR).
  2. **Portfolio Health Metrics**: Live occupancy rates, collection arrears, maintenance SLAs, and tenant retention percentages.
  3. **ERP & Accounting Integration**: Automated synchronization with enterprise systems (Yardi, SAP, RealPage, Zoho Books).
